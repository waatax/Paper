## 4. Raw Material Dynamics: Wood Pulp & Recovered Paper

In wood pulp markets, global hardwood pulp market leader Suzano issued a formal price increase letter for October shipments (+USD 20/MT), adamantly defending Asian and global hardwood pricing thresholds. Concurrently, Barclays downgraded European forest products group UPM, arguing that massive new capacity additions from UPM Paso de los Toros (2.1M MT/yr) and Suzano Cerrado (2.55M MT/yr) will continue exerting structural pressure on market pulp balances. In China, imported NBSK spot quotes held firm at USD 693/MT (RMB 4,930), with BHKP steady at USD 627/MT (RMB 4,465). Mainstream port inventories at Qingdao and Changshu declined for the 13th consecutive week to 2.065M tonnes, as downstream printing and packaging paper mills maintained high seasonal run rates of 71.0%. The NBSK-BHKP price spread stabilized at RMB 465/MT ($66/MT), comfortably below the 500 RMB threshold, eliminating fiber substitution incentives.

In recovered fiber, US domestic OCC collection and collection channels operated in steady balance, with export prices holding at USD 135–137/short ton FAS West Coast and USD 137–139/short ton FAS East Coast. China domestic Grade A OCC collection prices held steady at RMB 2,025/ton as sorting centers resumed operations after the Golden Week holidays. In Taiwan, mill gate OCC intake prices remained balanced at NT$4,200/ton, supported by strong autumn box shipments across Cheng Loong and Long Chen facilities.

## 5. Finished Paper Markets: Containerboard, Cultural & Special Paper

In industrial containerboard, North American producers PCA, Smurfit Westrock, and International Paper completed their first full month of higher invoice billings following the September 1 price increase, anchoring the Raw Material Spread at an unprecedented $840/short ton ($975 linerboard vs. $135 OCC). Concurrently, Smurfit Westrock and Pratt Industries continue advancing USITC and DOC anti-dumping and countervailing duty petitions against unfairly priced imported corrugated pizza boxes. In ivory board and specialty paper, China's leading mills—Nine Dragons, Sinar Mas APP, Bo Hui, and Liansheng—achieved full invoicing realization for their +RMB 200/ton price increase effective October 1, firming food-grade FBB factory prices at RMB 4,350–4,550/ton and ending multi-quarter operating losses. Spurred by the final 9-day countdown to the EU PFHxA prohibition and ongoing enforcement of China's GB 4806.10 coating standard, export orders for PFAS-free greaseproof paper and aqueous dispersion barrier cardboards surged, fueling high converting runs across Chung Hwa Pulp and YFY.
