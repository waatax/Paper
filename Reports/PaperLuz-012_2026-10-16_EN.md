## 1. Executive Summary & Key Strategic Insights

1. **Taiwan Big 4 Paper Mills Complete September Revenue Releases: YFY Hits NT$6.65B and CHP Posts NT$1.52B Ahead of Peak Q4**: YFY Inc. (1907) reported September revenue of NT$6.653B (+3.95% YoY, +3.10% MoM), bringing 1–9M cumulative revenue to NT$57.415B (+4.87% YoY) with containerboard growth across Taiwan, Vietnam, and China. Chung Hwa Pulp (1905) posted September revenue of NT$1.520B (+0.07% MoM), noting that September's +$20/MT hardwood pulp hike typically transmits to paper prices within two months, while high European energy costs give CHP an export edge. Alongside Cheng Loong (NT$4.178B, +17.08% YoY) and Long Chen (NT$4.790B, +25.29% YoY; 9M pre-tax profit NT$599M, EPS NT$0.46), all four mills are poised for Q4 seasonal strength.
2. **EU PFHxA Prohibition Enters Final 48-Hour Countdown (Effective October 18); Non-Fluorinated Barrier Coatings Take Full Command**: Regulation (EU) 2024/2462 under REACH Annex XVII prohibiting perfluorohexanoic acid (PFHxA) and related substances in food contact paper packaging becomes mandatory on October 18, 2026. European customs and supermarket chains have completed final inventory clearance, driving full-capacity shipments of PFAS-free aqueous barrier boards from CHP (CircuWell) and Taiwanese converters.
3. **Maine Water Utility Files Federal Lawsuit Against Huhtamaki, Sappi, and Kimberly-Clark Over PFAS Contamination**: The Greater Augusta Utility District in Maine filed a federal lawsuit in mid-October alleging that decades of wastewater and sludge discharges from Huhtamaki, Sappi North America, and Kimberly-Clark along the Kennebec River contaminated local wells with PFAS (23.3 ppt vs. Maine's 20 ppt limit), forcing a $2M ion-exchange treatment upgrade. This escalates PFAS risk from product bans to historic environmental liability litigation.
4. **China Containerboard Majors Launch Mid-October Price Hikes (+RMB 50/MT); Long Chen & YFY Follow Suit for Singles' Day Restocking**: Following early-October adjustments, Chinese containerboard leaders led by Nine Dragons initiated a second wave of +RMB 50/MT price increases in mid-October. Taiwanese mills operating in China, including Long Chen and YFY, followed suit to stimulate downstream box plant restocking ahead of the Double 11 e-commerce festival.
5. **International Paper (IP) Pursues Dual-Track for EMEA Packaging Business: London Listing Alongside ~$6 Billion PE Buyout Talks**: IP confirmed on October 15 that while progressing with the planned spin-off of its EMEA Packaging business (combining DS Smith operations) ahead of a November 3 Capital Markets Day, it is actively evaluating unsolicited buyout inquiries from private equity firms Lone Star and Atlas Holdings valued near $6 billion, sending IP shares up over 7%.
6. **South American Megaproject Divergence: Arauco's 3.5M MT Sucuriú Mill Reaches 81% Completion While CMPC's $4.6B Project Faces Rating Scrutiny**: Chile's Arauco reported that its $4.6B Sucuriú project in Brazil—the world's largest single-line hardwood pulp mill (3.5M MT/yr)—has reached 81% completion ahead of schedule for a Q4 2027 startup. Conversely, CMPC's $4.6B Natureza project in Brazil faces credit-rating pressure over leverage concerns.
7. **UPM Implements October Curtailments at Pietarsaari Mill and Inaugurates Pioneering Leuna Biorefinery in Germany**: To balance high Nordic wood costs, UPM planned temporary October production curtailments at its Pietarsaari pulp mill in Finland, supporting Suzano's +$50/MT October hike in Europe/Americas (reference price ~$1,360/MT). Simultaneously, UPM officially inaugurated its commercial wood-based biochemicals biorefinery in Leuna, Germany.
8. **Nippon Paper Debuts "Non-Aluminum Fuji-Pak" with Internal Printing Technology in Partnership with Ito En**: On October 14, Nippon Paper unveiled its eco-friendly "Non-Aluminum Fuji-Pak" liquid carton using a transparent barrier film instead of aluminum foil, enabling ambient shelf life and standard milk-carton recycling. Removing silver foil enabled an industry-first "Internal Printing Technology," commercialized with Ito En's vegetable juice to display fortune-telling ("Vege-Mikuji") graphics inside flattened cartons to incentivize consumer recycling.
9. **Australian Supermarket Giant Coles Hikes Paper Shopping Bag Prices by 20% to 30 Cents Amid Cost Pressures**: Major Australian grocer Coles quietly raised checkout paper bag prices from 25 cents to 30 cents (+20%, above Woolworths and Aldi at 25c), citing rising raw material and transport costs—demonstrating strong retail cost pass-through for high-tensile sack kraft bags under plastic bans.
10. **Brent Crude Drops 6.0% to $74.45/bbl; Taiwan Mills Reach 48.8% Biomass SRF Substitution to Cut Steam Cost to NT$718/Ton**: Easing Middle East risk premiums and softer IEA demand forecasts pushed Brent crude down to $74.45/bbl (-19% over two weeks), relieving latex, AKD sizing, and bunker surcharge pressures. Meanwhile, YFY and Cheng Loong reaffirmed on Oct 15 that domestic consumer tissue prices will remain frozen.

## 4. Raw Material Dynamics: Wood Pulp & Recovered Paper

Global wood pulp markets exhibited firm spot pricing alongside active producer supply management and major long-term project updates. In China, imported NBSK spot prices edged up to USD 694/MT (RMB 4,935), while BHKP firmed to USD 628/MT (RMB 4,470) and SHFE NBSK futures settled at RMB 4,872/MT (USD 685). Mainstream port inventories at Qingdao and Changshu recorded their 14th consecutive weekly net outflow, dropping to 2.052M tonnes. On the supply side, four major developments shaped the landscape: first, **Suzano** implemented October list price increases of **+$50/MT in Europe and the Americas** (lifting European eucalyptus reference prices to ~$1,360/MT) and **+$20/MT in Asia**; second, **UPM** scheduled temporary October production curtailments at its **Pietarsaari pulp mill in Finland** to optimize wood sourcing following its Kaukas shutdown; third, **Stora Enso** is executing the permanent H2 2026 closure of its softwood pulp line (Fiberline 3) at Skutskär, Sweden while investing €19M to expand fluff pulp capacity by 10%; fourth, Chile's **Arauco** reported that its $4.6B **Sucuriú grassroot pulp mill in Brazil (3.5M MT/yr single-line BHKP)** has reached **81% completion** ahead of schedule for a Q4 2027 startup, whereas rival **CMPC** faces credit-rating pressure over its $4.6B Brazilian pulp project.

In recovered fiber, US 11# OCC held firm at USD 135–137/short ton FAS West Coast and USD 137–139/short ton FAS East Coast. In China, mid-October containerboard price hike announcements (+RMB 50/MT) lifted domestic Grade A OCC quotes by RMB 10 to RMB 2,035/ton (USD 287/ton). Taiwan domestic OCC mill gate prices remained stable at NT$4,200/ton (USD 130.9/ton) amid steady Q4 intake across all four major mills.
