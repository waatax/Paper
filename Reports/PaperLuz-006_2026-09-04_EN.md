## 1. 10-Point Executive Summary & Strategic Insights

1. **North American Containerboard 9/1 Price Hikes Enacted with Dominant Producer Pricing Power**: PCA (+$140/short ton), Smurfit Westrock (+$100/short ton), and IP (+$80/short ton) have formally executed their third-round price increases in September invoices. 2. **Suzano Raises Asian Pulp Prices by $20/ton, Concluding 5-Month Bottom Consolidation**: Global pulp leader Suzano announced a $20/metric ton price hike for China and Asian markets effective September 2026—the first price hike in Asia since April. The move reflects complete global absorption of Cerrado's 2.55M ton supply and low buyer inventories, propelling BHKP spot to 4,420 RMB/ton (USD 615–620).
3. **China Imported NBSK Spot Firms to 4,865 RMB/ton on "Golden September" Industrial Demand**: Port pulp inventories across Qingdao and Changshu declined for the eighth consecutive week to 2.148M tonnes. Downstream cultural and packaging mill operating rates reached 68.5%, while SHFE pulp futures settled at 4,786 RMB/ton with cash-futures basis tightly anchored at +79 RMB/ton.
4. **NBSK - BHKP Spread Contracts to 445 RMB/ton ($62), Affirming Healthy Fiber Co-movement**: Faster hardwood recovery (+55 RMB) against softwood (+45 RMB) converged the spread to 445 RMB/ton, remaining comfortably under the 500 RMB substitution tipping point and eliminating down-trading risks.
5. **Brent Crude Steady at $94.65/bbl, Cementing High Chemical and Freight Surcharge Floors**: Enforcement of the US blockade on Iranian crude keeps Singapore VLSFO elevated, sustaining maritime Bunker Surcharges (BSS); AKD surface sizing, SBR latex, and PE extrusion coatings maintain year-to-date highs.
6. **Cheng Loong (1904) Reports H1 Net Profit of NT$1.602B (Up 52x YoY), with EPS of NT$1.45**: Ramping of Ping Duong Phase 3 (400k MT/yr) pushed group annual capacity past 1M tons in Vietnam, capturing high-margin export orders displaced by elevated US containerboard pricing.
7. **Long Chen (1909) Awarded Taiwan Sustainable Action Award, 7-Month Pre-Tax Profit Hits NT$429M**: High operating rates (>95%) across Jiangsu and Hubei mills and proprietary waste heat recovery systems deliver double dividends in carbon reduction and production cost stabilization.
8. **China GB 4806.10-2025 Food Contact Coatings Standard Officially in Effect (Sept 2)**: The revised national standard formally covers paper coatings, imposing strict migration limits on primary aromatic amines, BPA, and heavy metals while accelerating PFAS substitution nationwide.
9. **EU PPWR Active for 23 Days; Rigorous PFAS Audits Accelerate Aqueous Barrier Shift**: European customs actively inspect food packaging for total fluorine (<50 ppm) and Design for Recycling (DfR), triggering strong European and Asian demand for certified non-PFAS aqueous coated board from YFY and Chung Hwa Pulp.
10. **Taiwan Mills' Solid Recovered Fuel (SRF) Replacement Exceeds 46%, Anchoring Steam at NT$735/ton**: Internal circular biomass cogeneration allows Taiwanese mills to decouple steam costs from volatile grid tariffs, creating a distinct margin moat over gas-dependent overseas rivals.
