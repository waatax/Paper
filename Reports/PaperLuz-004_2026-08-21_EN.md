## I. Executive 10-Line Summary & Key Insights

1. **Aug 20: US Enacts Comprehensive Economic Blockade & Secondary Sanctions on Iran, Brent Crude Surges Past $93.80**: The US Treasury launched "Operation Economic Fury", combining Persian Gulf tanker interdictions and secondary banking sanctions. Tanker charter rates and war risk premiums spiked, immediately elevating marine bunker fuel surcharges (BSS) and petrochemical papermaking inputs (SBR latex, extrusion PE).
2. **Proprietary Biomass (SRF) Co-Generation Serves as Critical Energy Moat**: Amid fossil energy inflation, Taiwanese mills (Cheng Loong, YFY, Long Chen) leverage >40-50% solid recovered fuel (SRF) substitution, locking steam costs at $22.5–24.2/MT (NT$720–780) to insulate margins compared to grid-dependent European and Chinese competitors.
3. **Suzano Q2 Net Price at $601/MT Establishes Global Hardwood Floor**: European structural hardwood deficits kept regional quotes at $1,180–1,290/MT, while Chinese buying at $690–750/MT returned steadily, offsetting volume from the 2.55M MT Cerrado mega-project.
4. **Long Chen (1909.TW) Announces Spin-Off into Investment Holding Company**: The board approved spinning off Taiwan paper and carton operations into subsidiary "Long Chen Low-Carbon Packaging", restructuring the parent as an Investment Holding Company (effective Jan 4, 2027). Benefiting from 15% cumulative Chinese containerboard price increases, 7-month pre-tax profit reached $13.3M USD (NT$429M).
5. **Cheng Loong (1904.TW) Vietnam Phase 3 Powers July Revenue to 19-Month Peak**: July sales hit $132.0M USD (NT$4.26B, +9.75% MoM, +22.82% YoY) as Binh Duong capacity exceeded 1.0M MT. Full realization of domestic +10% hikes extends Q1 profit growth (+588.8% YoY) into H2.
6. **YFY H1 Net Profit Hits $24.1M USD (NT$779M); Chung Hwa Pulp Defends Pricing**: YFY turned profitable on Vietnam containerboard volume and a $33.8M USD dividend contribution from E-Ink. Chung Hwa Pulp implements "production curtailment to defend pricing" through October while expanding plastic-free barrier board.
7. **North American Containerboard Sept 1 Hikes in Final 11-Day Countdown**: PCA (+$140/t), Smurfit Westrock (+$100/t), and IP (+$80/t) take effect Sept 1. Mill Raw Material Spreads reached a 3-year record high of $720/short ton, with geopolitical energy surges reinforcing mill resolve despite AICC oligopoly protests.
8. **Virgin Wood Pulp Completes Bottoming; Futures Basis Closes for Autumn Restocking**: China NBSK spot prices held at $690–750/MT (RMB 4,950/t), while SHFE futures stabilized at $660/MT (RMB 4,730/t). Port inventories declined for the 6th consecutive week to 2.205M MT.
9. **EU PPWR Law Formally in Force for 9 Days**: Mandatory since 2026-08-12. Food-contact packaging PFAS restrictions (25 ppb limit) are strictly enforced without transitional grace periods for existing inventory.
10. **China GB 4806.10 Food Coating Standard Countdown at 12 Days; EU PFHxA at 51 Days**: Food board, paper cups, and takeout boxes are rapidly eliminating fluorinated coatings, driving a historic surge in orders for aqueous bio-based barrier paper.
