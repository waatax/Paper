## I. Executive 10-Line Summary & Key Insights

1. **EU PPWR Enters Final 5-Day Countdown**: Mandatory starting 2026-08-12. All packaging imported into the EU must satisfy Design for Recycling (DfR) and Digital Product Passport (DPP) compliance. Non-compliant packaging faces border detention and heavy eco-fees.

3. **Top 5 Paper Packaging Mega-Trends Established for 2026**: (1) Paper-over-plastic food barrier substitution; (2) DfR mono-material lightweighting; (3) Aqueous dispersion barrier coatings replacing PE extrusion; (4) DPP lifecycle traceability; (5) High-barrier unbleached kraft paper growth.
4. **North American Containerboard Firm Seller's Market Solidifies**: PCA (+$140/short ton), IP (+$80/t), and Smurfit Westrock (+$100/t) Sept 1 price adjustments enter final preparation. Americas permanent mill closures of 3.9M MT keep inventories tight and order backlogs extended.
5. **Virgin Wood Pulp Consolidates at Cost Floor**: China NBSK spot prices held at $672/MT (RMB 4,800/t) with BHKP at $625/MT (RMB 4,480/t). The $47/MT spread maintains bottom price discipline, stopping further paper mill hardwood substitution.
6. **US OCC Export Prices Stable, Raw Material Spread Reaches $720/t**: US #11 OCC export FAS held steady at $130–135/short ton. With recovered fiber prices suppressed, containerboard mills enjoy historic gross profit margins.
7. **Long Chen Secures H1 Turnaround**: Driven by Chinese packaging price recovery (+15% since April) and lean recovered paper procurement, Long Chen recorded profitable H1 pre-tax earnings and prepares strategic corporate restructuring.
8. **Cheng Loong Expands Southeast Asian Leadership**: Cheng Loong's Vietnam Binh Duong Phase 3 (400k MT) operates near full capacity, cementing its position as Vietnam's largest containerboard producer with H2 gross margin trajectory pointing to 18–20%.
9. **Energy & Steam Cost Containment in Asian Mills**: Taiwan thermal coal CIF prices softened to $142.5/MT, with major papermakers leveraging >40% solid recovered fuel (SRF) biomass power to cap steam costs at $22.5–24.2/MT (NT$720–780).
10. **China GB 4806.10 Food Coating Standard Countdown at 26 Days**: Mandating strict migration limits on food packaging coatings effective Sept 2, accelerating the phase-out of traditional fluorinated food boxes.

## IV. Top 5 Paper Packaging Mega-Trends for 2026

1. **Plastic-to-Paper Acceleration**: Global FMCG brands are accelerating replacement of single-use plastics in food service, electronics, and e-commerce packaging.
2. **Design for Recycling (DfR) Optimization**: Minimizing mixed-material laminates to ensure >95% fiber yield in standard mechanical repulping systems without specialized chemical delamination.
3. **PFAS-Free Water-Based Dispersion Coatings**: High-performance aqueous barrier boards replacing traditional PE extrusion coatings and fluorinated oil-resistant treatments.
4. **Digital Product Passport (DPP) Integration**: QR codes and digital identifiers providing full lifecycle traceability of fiber origin, recycled content, and carbon intensity.
5. **High-Strength Unbleached Kraft Paper Growth**: Surge in retail and food-delivery demand driving premium unbleached virgin and high-strength recycled kraft bag papers.
