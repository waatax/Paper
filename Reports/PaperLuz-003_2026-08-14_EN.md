## I. Executive 10-Line Summary & Key Insights

1. **North American Containerboard Sept 1 Price Hikes Fully Realized**: PCA (+$140/short ton), IP (+$80/t), and Smurfit Westrock (+$100/t) enter final execution. The cumulative 3.9M MT permanent Americas mill closures keep supply exceptionally tight, pushing Raw Material Spreads past $720/short ton.
2. **Western vs. Asian Containerboard Price Spread Expands to $180–220/MT**: European containerboard surged +€120/MT in Q2 with most grades sold out, prompting Asian giants (Nine Dragons, Lee & Man, Cheng Loong) to capture export orders across the Middle East, India, and Southeast Asia.
3. **Virgin Wood Pulp Completes Bottoming, Spread Consolidates at $61/MT**: China imported NBSK spot averaged RMB 4,783/MT ($668/MT), while BHKP hardwood stood at RMB 4,350/MT ($607/MT). The $61/MT spread remains well below the $70 threshold, sustaining strong base demand for softwood ahead of autumn peak restocking.
4. **Cheng Loong Vietnam Expansion Drives July Revenue to 19-Month High**: July revenue reached $132.0M USD (NT$4.26B, +9.74% MoM, +22.82% YoY) as Binh Duong Phase 3 (400k MT) ramped up to over 1.0M MT total Vietnam capacity; H2 gross margins projected at 18–20%.
5. **Long Chen 7-Month Pre-Tax Profit Hits $13.3M USD (NT$429M)**: July revenue reached $134.1M USD (NT$4.32B, +20.32% YoY) with single-month pre-tax profit of $4.0M USD. Benefiting from China's anti-involution pricing discipline, Long Chen suspended trading on Aug 12 ahead of a major corporate restructuring announcement.
6. **Energy & Freight Cost Linkage: Papermaking Costs Well-Controlled**: Brent crude settled at $80.12/bbl and Taiwan thermal coal CIF declined to $142.0/MT. Combined with Taiwanese mills' >40% solid recovered fuel (SRF) biomass substitution, steam costs locked at $22.5–24.2/MT (NT$720–780). SCFI stood at 3,276 pts.
7. **Full-Chain Forward Matrix (3.6) Established**: 1M/3M/6M econometric modeling indicates that with >80% containerboard price hike execution, Asian paper manufacturers' operating margins will expand by 120–180 bps in H2.
8. **EU PPWR Regulation (EU) 2025/40 Formally in Effect (Day 2)**: Mandatory since 2026-08-12. All packaging imported into the EU is subject to Design for Recycling (DfR) and Digital Product Passport (DPP) audits; non-compliant materials face severe border tariffs.
9. **Strong Demand for Kraft Bag Paper in Japan and North America**: Novolex in the US alongside The Pack (3950.T) and Super Bag (3945.T) in Japan report robust unbleached kraft bag shipments driven by municipal plastic bag bans and takeout packaging upgrades.
10. **China GB 4806.10 Food Coating Standard Countdown at 19 Days; EU PFHxA at 58 Days**: Food boxboard, extrusion cups, and greaseproof cartons accelerating the complete elimination of fluorochemicals in favor of aqueous dispersion barriers.
