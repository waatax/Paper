## 1. Executive Summary & Key Strategic Insights

1. **Global Market Pulp Leader Suzano Announces Additional +$50/MT Hardwood Pulp Hike for Europe & Americas Effective November 1, Lifting European List Price to $1,410/MT**: Between October 28 and 29, Brazilian pulp giant Suzano officially notified global customers of a fresh +$50/MT price increase for bleached eucalyptus kraft pulp (BEKP/BHKP) in Europe and the Americas effective November 1, raising the European CIF benchmark to $1,410/MT while signaling an additional +$20/MT hike for Asian markets. Driven by European seasonal restocking, Nordic mill curtailments, and 16 consecutive weeks of Chinese port inventory drawdowns, the move cements an upward Q4 pricing floor across global fiber markets.
2. **China's Integrated Leader Sun Paper (002078.SZ) Posts Stellar Q3 Earnings, with 9M Net Profit Up +16.88% YoY and Q3 Net Profit Reaching RMB 921M**: Sun Paper released its Q3 2026 financial report on October 28, outperforming consensus across both revenue and net income. Nine-month net profit attributable to shareholders rose +16.88% YoY. The results validate Sun Paper's "Laos Forest-Pulp-Paper Base + Shandong & Guangxi Dual Hubs" strategy, where high woodchip and virgin pulp self-sufficiency insulated margins against raw material fluctuations.
3. **North American Packaging Earnings Divergence: Smurfit Westrock (SW) Plummets 10% on Q3 Miss and Cost Inflation, Contrasting Sharply with PCA's Cash Conversion**: Smurfit Westrock reported Q3 earnings on October 28 that missed Wall Street expectations due to "unprecedented cost inflation" in fiber, energy, and labor alongside post-merger integration friction, sending shares down 10%. In stark contrast to Packaging Corp of America's (PKG) stellar Q3 beat (+5.2% shipment growth, 93% mill operating rate) the prior week, the divergence proves that domestically focused, highly modernized US mills convert the $839/ST raw spread into cash flow far more effectively than sprawling transatlantic conglomerates.
4. **International Paper (IP) Holds Q3 Earnings Call Ahead of Nov 3 Capital Markets Day, Accelerating EMEA Packaging Divestiture**: During its October 30 Q3 earnings call, International Paper highlighted persistent European energy and restructuring headwinds, confirming that management is accelerating the dual-track separation of its EMEA Packaging unit (former DS Smith assets)—weighing an independent London Stock Exchange listing against ~$6B private equity buyout bids from Lone Star and Atlas Holdings—to concentrate capital on high-margin North American containerboard.
5. **North American Bleached Board Shakeup: Clearwater Paper Flags Operational Bottlenecks at Acquired Augusta Mill**: As reported by *Packaging Dive* on October 27, Clearwater Paper disclosed unexpected equipment failures and operational bottlenecks at its Augusta, Georgia bleached paperboard (SBS) mill—acquired earlier from Graphic Packaging—prompting management to evaluate strategic restructuring options and tightening North American folding carton board supply.
6. **EU PFHxA Ban Enters Week 2 of Mandatory Customs Enforcement; CHP CircuWell Booked into 2027Q1 as Zhongxin Accelerates 50k-MT Indonesia Molded Fiber Hub**: Entering the second week of mandatory enforcement under Regulation (EU) 2024/2462 (effective October 18), European ports including Rotterdam and Hamburg are actively screening food contact paper for the 25 ppb PFHxA limit. Chung Hwa Pulp's (CHP 1905) fluorine-free CircuWell barrier board now enjoys order visibility into Q1 2027, while Chinese molded fiber leader Zhongxin (603091.SH) accelerated construction of its 50,000-MT bagasse tableware base in Indonesia to bypass Western tariffs and capture plastic-ban demand.
7. **China Imported Virgin Pulp Spot Prices Step Up as Main Port Stocks Fall for 16th Consecutive Week to 2.018M MT**: Bolstered by Suzano's November hike announcement, China imported NBSK spot rose +RMB 20/MT to USD 700/MT (RMB 4,960/MT), while imported BHKP spot climbed +RMB 15/MT to USD 632/MT (RMB 4,490/MT), maintaining a healthy NBSK-BHKP spread of USD 68/MT (RMB 470/MT). Mainstream port inventories fell for the 16th straight week to 2.018M MT (-22k MT WoW). Meanwhile, French environmental giant Veolia won the industrial water treatment contract for Arauco's 3.5M-MT Sucuriú pulp project in Brazil, and Sweden's Södra opened a new US sales office near Boston.
8. **Double 11 E-Commerce Packaging Reaches Peak Velocity; Cross-Strait Containerboard Hikes (+RMB 100/MT) Stick and Lift Domestic OCC to RMB 2,050/MT**: Late October marked the final box-making sprint for the Singles' Day shopping festival. Two cumulative waves of +RMB 100/MT containerboard hikes by Nine Dragons, Shanying, Long Chen (1909), and YFY (1907) were fully billed, lifting secondary box plant utilization to 86% and pushing China Grade-A domestic OCC up +RMB 5/MT to RMB 2,050/MT (USD 288/MT).
9. **Brent Crude Builds Base at $73.80/bbl as Taiwan Mills Lift SRF Biomass Substitution to 49.2%, Locking Steam Cost at NT$718/t**: ICE Brent crude futures settled at $73.80/bbl (WTI at $70.10/bbl), preserving lower chemical costs for AKD sizing, SBR latex, and PE extrusion resins. Simultaneously, Taiwan's Big 4 paper mills elevated Solid Recovered Fuel (SRF) biomass co-firing to 49.2%, locking co-generation steam costs at a historic low of NT$718/ton ($22.3/MT).
10. **Container Freight Index (SCFI) Eases to 3,310 Points with Smooth Holiday Schedules; USD/TWD at 32.150 Enhances Q4 Export FX Gains**: The Shanghai Containerized Freight Index (SCFI) edged down 0.5% WoW to 3,310.20 points (USWC $6,390/FEU, USEC $9,180/FEU) and the Baltic Dry Index (BDI) settled at 1,830 points, with vessel schedule reliability above 85%. The New Taiwan Dollar closed at 32.150 per USD (-3 cents WoW), providing favorable FX translation for Cheng Loong's Vietnam packaging exports and CHP's European specialty paper sales.

## 4. Papermaking Raw Materials: Pulp & Recovered Paper

The global woodpulp market delivered a major bullish catalyst in the final week of October: **world-leading market pulp producer Suzano officially announced on October 28–29 a fresh +$50/MT price increase for bleached eucalyptus kraft pulp (BEKP) across Europe and the Americas effective November 1**, lifting the European CIF reference list price to **USD 1,410/MT** and signaling further upward adjustments for Asia. Following the full realization of September–October hikes, international pulp producers have firmly seized Q4 pricing power through disciplined supply management and European seasonal restocking.

Buoyed by overseas list hikes, China imported Northern Bleached Softwood Kraft (NBSK) spot breached the $700 threshold to reach **USD 700/MT (RMB 4,960/MT, +RMB 20 WoW)**, while Bleached Hardwood Kraft Pulp (BHKP) spot climbed to **USD 632/MT (RMB 4,490/MT, +RMB 15 WoW)**. On the Shanghai Futures Exchange (SHFE), the active softwood pulp futures contract surged +RMB 27/MT to settle at **RMB 4,905/MT (USD 689/MT)**, maintaining a healthy spot-futures basis of +RMB 55/MT (+USD 11/MT) and a balanced NBSK-BHKP spread of RMB 470/MT (USD 68/MT). Mainstream port inventories at Qingdao and Changshu recorded their **16th consecutive weekly drawdown, falling 22,000 MT to 2.018M MT**. Concurrently, French environmental services leader **Veolia won the industrial water treatment contract for Arauco's 3.5M-MT Sucuriú pulp mill in Brazil** (now 82.5% complete), while Swedish forestry group **Södra opened a North American sales office near Boston** to expand direct pulp and timber sales on the US East Coast.

In recovered fiber markets, US 11# OCC held steady at USD 135–137/ST FAS on the West Coast and USD 137–139/ST FAS on the East Coast. In mainland China, peak Double 11 e-commerce box deliveries drove active mill consumption, lifting Grade-A domestic OCC mill-gate prices by RMB 5/MT to **RMB 2,050/MT (USD 288/MT)**. Taiwan domestic mill-gate OCC prices remained stable at **NT$4,200/MT (USD 130.6/MT)**.

## 5. Finished Paper Markets: Containerboard, Cultural & Specialty Papers

North American paper and packaging markets witnessed a dramatic **Q3 earnings divergence** this week. On October 28, transatlantic packaging giant **Smurfit Westrock (NYSE: SW)** reported Q3 results that fell short of Wall Street expectations, triggering a **10% single-day stock plunge**. Management cited "unprecedented cost inflation" across fiber, energy, and labor at older mill assets, compounded by post-merger integration friction and a workplace fatality investigation in Virginia. This stood in sharp contrast to **Packaging Corporation of America (NYSE: PKG)**, which beat Q3 estimates the prior week on $2.18B in sales and a 93% operating rate—proving that under the same $975/ST linerboard price and $839/ST raw spread, highly modernized, domestically focused US mills convert pricing into cash flow far more efficiently than complex cross-border consolidators.

Simultaneously, **International Paper (NYSE: IP)** held its Q3 earnings call on October 30, addressing structural cost initiatives ahead of its **November 3 Capital Markets Day** and confirming the accelerated dual-track separation of its EMEA Packaging business (former DS Smith)—evaluating a London Stock Exchange spin-off alongside ~$6B private equity takeover bids from Lone Star and Atlas Holdings. Meanwhile, **Clearwater Paper (NYSE: CLW)** disclosed on October 27 that unexpected equipment failures at its newly acquired Augusta, Georgia bleached paperboard mill weighed on earnings, prompting a strategic review and tightening North American SBS folding carton supply.

In Asia, **Sun Paper (002078.SZ) reported standout Q3 financials on October 28, with Q3 net profit reaching RMB 921M and 9M net profit surging +16.88% YoY**, demonstrating the cost moat of its integrated Laos forest-pulp-paper complex. Across Greater China, two cumulative waves of +RMB 100/MT containerboard hikes by Nine Dragons, Shanying, Long Chen (1909), and YFY (1907) were fully realized during the Double 11 peak, while **Chung Hwa Pulp (1905)** gained further Q4 paper pricing momentum from Suzano's November +$50/MT pulp hike.
