4. Feedstock Market Dynamics: Wood Pulp & Recovered Fiber

In market pulp, Brazilian hardwood leader Suzano announced a new round of price increases for October, firmly establishing price defense across Asian and global markets. Nordic and North American softwood producers reinforced export allocation discipline, nudging imported NBSK spot quotes to USD 694/ton (RMB 4,925/ton). Port stocks at Qingdao and Changshu declined for the twelfth consecutive week to 2.072M tonnes, underscoring resilient intake across cultural, tissue, and ivory board mills (~70.5% operating rates). The NBSK-BHKP spot spread held at RMB 465/ton ($66/ton), well below the 500 RMB threshold, eliminating fiber furnish substitution risks.

In recovered fiber, US domestic box collection remained well balanced, with export FAS quotes holding steady at $135–$137/short ton on the West Coast and $137–$139/short ton on the East Coast. In China, Grade A OCC mill gate prices ticked up slightly to RMB 2,025/ton ahead of golden week holidays; Taiwan domestic mill gate prices held flat at NT$4,200/ton ($131.2/ton).

5. Finished Products: Containerboard, Cultural & Specialty Papers

In containerboard, North American producers PCA, Smurfit Westrock, and International Paper completed their first full month of invoicing at higher price levels, sustaining the record raw material spread of $840/short ton. Packaging Corporation of America (PCA) announced on October 2 the closure of its Middletown, Ohio corrugated plant to eliminate high-cost capacity and streamline converting operations. Concurrently, Smurfit Westrock and Pratt Industries continue their anti-dumping petition against unfairly priced imported pizza boxes.

In ivory board and specialty grades, Nine Dragons, APP, Bo Hui, and Liansheng achieved complete billing realization for their +RMB 200/MT hike starting October 1, bringing FBB spot quotes to RMB 4,350–4,550/MT ($638/MT). Meanwhile, the 16-day countdown to the EU PFHxA prohibition and China's GB 4806.10 entering its second month triggered urgent procurement waves for non-fluorinated aqueous barrier coatings and PFAS-free cardboards.

6. Special Feature: Global & Taiwan Food Packaging Insights (Paper Bags, Cups, Molded Fiber, Folding Cartons)

Executive Summary: With the EU PFHxA ban entering its final 16-day countdown and international plastic phase-outs expanding, sustainable food packaging is experiencing an unprecedented structural upgrade. This column provides an in-depth analysis of Taiwan's domestic supply chain while scanning China, Japan, the United States, New Zealand, Australia, and Southeast Asia.

6.1 🇹🇼 Taiwan Market Focus: Tissue Price Stability, Export Packaging & PFAS-Free Conversions

Major Mills Clarify Zero Tissue Price Hikes: YFY (Mayflower, Delight) and Cheng Loong (Spring Breeze, Dandelion) officially stated on October 1 that raw fiber inventories and converting efficiency are fully optimized, confirming zero price increases for retail tissue products in Q4 2026, reassuring consumers and stabilizing market sentiment.

Chung Hwa Pulp (1905): CHP's CircuWell non-plastic, non-fluorinated food board carries German PTS paper recyclability certification (>95% fiber yield) and DIN CERTCO biodegradability certification. Ahead of the Oct 18 EU deadline, CHP has secured international rush orders for grease-resistant burger wraps and bakery bags.

Cheng Loong (1904): Supplies heavy-duty kraft paper shopping bags (20–25 kgf tensile) for hypermarkets and convenience stores. Its 1M MT Binh Duong mill in Vietnam operates at full capacity, with northern and southern box plants expanding to serve major athletic footwear and electronics exporters.

Regional Converters (GPPC, Fulikang, Yongchun, Guangyuan): Central Taiwan converting hubs are accelerating installations of high-speed aqueous coating cup machines and automated SOS bag lines, fully phasing out fluorinated grease-proofing agents in favor of KIT 8–9 non-PFAS coatings.

Bakery & Breakfast Bags: Retail bakery chains and breakfast establishments are systematically transitioning away from wax-coated and plastic bags to non-PFAS kraft greaseproof bags (40–50g).

6.2 🇨🇳 China Market Scan: Full Realization of Ivory Board Hikes & Molded Fiber Boom

+RMB 200/MT Price Hike Fully Settled: Effective October 1, Nine Dragons, APP, Bo Hui, and Liansheng successfully billed their +RMB 200/MT price increases, with FBB quotes reaching RMB 4,350–4,550/MT, ending multi-quarter operating deficits.

Molded Fiber Exports Surge: Zhejiang Zhibang, Shengquan Group, and YUTO Packaging saw overseas shipments of bagasse clamshells and coffee lids maintain >25% YoY growth, driven by EU PPWR and North American single-use plastic restrictions. Feedstock bagasse pulp board quotes held steady at RMB 3,880–4,050/MT.

GB 4806.10 Reaches 30-Day Milestone: Mandatory enforcement of China's food contact coating standard reached one month, driving aqueous dispersion barrier adoption past 55% in foodservice paperboard.

6.3 🇯🇵 Japan Market Scan: Nanocellulose (MFC/CNF) 2026-2036 Strategic Push & Convenience Stores

Commercial Nanocellulose Barriers: Newly published research on the 2026–2036 micro- and nanocellulose market highlights Nippon Paper (cellenpia®) and Oji Holdings (AUROVISCO) utilizing CNF/MFC barrier coatings to replace aluminum foil and fluorinated chemicals in high-barrier food packaging.

Convenience Store De-Plastification: Japan's top 3 chains (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart) continue phasing out single-use plastics, deploying non-PFAS kraft bags for hot fried snacks and testing molded fiber bento trays.

Paper Bag Leaders: The Pack (3950.T) and Shimojima (7482.T) reported a 14.5% YoY rise in high-tensile shopping bags and twisted paper handle bags, benefiting from tourism and retail demand.

6.4 🇺🇸 US Market Scan: QSR Fluorine Bans, Sabert Acquisition & Plant Consolidations

6.5 🇳🇿/🇦🇺 ANZ Market Scan: NZ Plastic Regulations & Australia APCO Mandates

6.6 🌏 Southeast Asia Market Scan: Indonesia Food Packaging Rules, SCGP & Vietnam
