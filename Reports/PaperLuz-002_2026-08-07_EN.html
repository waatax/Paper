III. Top 5 Paper Packaging Mega-Trends for 2026

1. Plastic-to-Paper Acceleration: Replacement of single-use plastics in food and e-commerce packaging.
2. Design for Recycling (DfR) Optimization: Minimizing mixed-material laminates to ensure >95% fiber yield in standard repulping systems.
3. PFAS-Free Water-Based Dispersion Coatings: High-performance aqueous barrier boards replacing traditional PE extrusion coatings.
4. Digital Product Passport (DPP) Integration: Full digital traceability of fiber origin and environmental footprint.
5. High-Strength Unbleached Kraft Paper Growth: Surge in retail and food-delivery demand driving premium unbleached packaging papers.
