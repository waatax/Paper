## 1. Executive Summary & Key Strategic Insights

1. **Taiwan Big 4 Mills Post Robust August Revenues; Cheng Loong Hits 20-Month High, Long Chen Surges ~25% YoY**: Driven by North American containerboard price arbitrage, Southeast Asian demand, and domestic mid-autumn festival produce and electronics packaging rushes, Cheng Loong (1904) posted August consolidated revenue of NT$4.34B (20-month peak). Long Chen (1909) recorded NT$4.41B (+24.8% YoY), lifting 8-month cumulative pre-tax profit to NT$495M. Chung Hwa Pulp (1905) reached NT$1.519B (+1.65% MoM), and YFY (1907) gained 7.5% MoM to NT$6.68B.
2. **North American Containerboard Sept 1 Price Hikes Fully Invoiced; Raw Material Spread Reaches All-Time Record of $840/short ton**: Packaging Corp of America (+$140), Smurfit Westrock (+$100), and International Paper (+$80) fully implemented third-round hikes on September 1 invoices. Benchmark kraftliner spot held firm at $960–$980/short ton. Deducting US OCC ($135–$137/short ton), the mill spread widened to $840, delivering historic operating margins.
3. **South American Pulp Majors Advance Deep-Water Port Infrastructure: CMPC Secures $300M Private Terminal License**: Chile's CMPC received official authorization to construct a $300M dedicated deep-water pulp terminal at Port of Itapoá in Santa Catarina, Brazil. With annual throughput exceeding 3.0M tonnes, this terminal bypasses congested public facilities, compressing transshipment demurrage and securing long-term freight sovereignty to Asia and Europe.
4. **APP Indonesia Flagship Indah Kiat (IKKP) Posts H1 2026 Net Profit Surge of 127.3% to $295M**: Sinar Mas APP's Indonesian subsidiary Indah Kiat Pulp & Paper reported H1 net earnings of $295M, more than doubling YoY, driven by strong volumes and price realization across Asian cultural and packaging board markets and hyper-competitive vertical fiber integration.
5. **Chinese Paper Giants Initiate Pre-Holiday "Downtime Maintenance" to Protect Price Floors and Clear Inventories**: Ahead of the Mid-Autumn Festival and Golden Week National Day holidays, Nine Dragons, Shanying, and Sun Paper scheduled 3 to 7 days of coordinated machine downtime across select corrugating medium and liner lines, curbing supply and defending August price increases of RMB 30–50/ton.
6. **Suzano's September Asian BHKP Price Hike of +$20/MT Fully Realized, Ending 5-Month Floor Consolidation**: Suzano's $20/MT hike for Asian eucalyptus pulp was fully accepted by buyers, lifting spot transaction levels to $625–$630/MT (RMB 4,440/t). This demonstrates that global demand has completely absorbed the 2.55M tonnes from Brazil's Cerrado mill, solidifying the market trough.
7. **China Imported NBSK Spot Firms to $688/MT (RMB 4,890/t) as Mainstream Port Stocks Drop for 9th Consecutive Week**: Combined inventories at Qingdao and Changshu ports fell to 2.115M tonnes. Downstream ivory board and woodfree printing mills ramped operating rates to 69.2%. SHFE bleached softwood pulp futures closed at RMB 4,810/t, maintaining a healthy basis spread of +RMB 80/t (+USD 11).
8. **Softwood-Hardwood Price Spread Anchored at RMB 450/ton ($63/MT), Well Below the 500 RMB Substitution Threshold**: The stable price gap prevents mills from re-engineering fiber furnish recipes to substitute hardwood for softwood, preserving structural balance and price parity across the pulp spectrum.
9. **China GB 4806.10 Standard in Force for 9 Days; EU PPWR 30-Day Milestone Intensifies PFAS Customs Audits**: With China regulating paper coatings under food-contact law and the EU inspecting food packaging for total fluorine (<50 ppm) and DfR recyclable design, inquiries for non-plastic, aqueous barrier foodboard from YFY and Chung Hwa Pulp continue to climb rapidly.
10. **Taiwan Mills' Solid Recovered Fuel (SRF) Biomass Replacement Exceeds 47%, Locking Steam Cost at NT$730/ton**: Faced with grid electricity tariff pressure and looming 2027 carbon fee declarations, Taiwanese paper mills expanded circular cogeneration, keeping steam costs over 30% below European gas-powered mills and constructing a formidable gross margin fortress.

## 4. Feedstock Market Dynamics: Wood Pulp & Recovered Fiber

Following Suzano's $20/MT hardwood pulp hike, market sentiment across Asia solidified rapidly. Nordic and North American softwood producers, including Metsa Board and Canfor, reinforced list price discipline, providing significant leverage for upcoming Q4 contract negotiations. Port stocks at Qingdao and Changshu dropped for the ninth consecutive week to 2.115M tonnes, underscoring resilient paper mill intake.

In recovered fiber, US domestic box collection remained well balanced, with export FAS quotes holding steady at $135–$137/short ton on the West Coast and $137–$139/short ton on the East Coast. In China, Grade A OCC mill gate prices ticked up slightly to RMB 2,015/ton as packing yards showed reluctance to sell ahead of the festive holidays.

In containerboard, North American producers PCA, Smurfit Westrock, and International Paper successfully billed their third round of increases effective September 1, amplifying international price arbitrage. ## 7. Global Regulations & ESG Transformation

- **EU PPWR 30 Days in Force**: European port authorities have commenced systematic sampling of food-contact paper packaging for total fluorine (<50 ppm) and verified Design for Recycling (DfR) declarations.
- **China GB 4806.10-2025 Enacted (Day 9)**: Paper coatings are officially governed under mandatory food safety standards, driving rapid market substitution away from fluorinated chemicals toward water-based bio-barrier coatings.
- **EU PFHxA Ban Final Countdown**: 37 days remaining (effective 2026-10-18). Perfluorohexanoic acid and related substances will be prohibited across EU food packaging.
- **EU EUDR Deforestation Regulation Countdown**: 110 days remaining (effective 2026-12-30). Wood pulp, paperboard, and cartons entering the EU require plot-level GPS coordinates and Due Diligence Statements (DDS).
- **Taiwan 2026 Carbon Fee Implementation**: Large industrial emitters prepare inaugural greenhouse gas filings. Cheng Loong, YFY, and Long Chen expand SRF biomass fuels (>47% replacement) and waste heat recovery to secure preferential fee discounts.
