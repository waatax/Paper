Geopolitical Energy US "Operation Economic Fury" on Iran Sustains $94 Oil, Cascading Chemical and Maritime Freight Surcharges: Hormuz strait military escorts and shipping insurance spikes drive Singapore VLSFO above $720/ton, prompting major container carriers to impose September Bunker Surcharges (BSS); AKD sizing, SBR latex, and PE extrusion coatings rise 6–8% MoM.

Energy Moat Proprietary Biomass (SRF) Co-generation Forms Unassailable Moat, Anchoring Mill Steam Cost at NT$740/ton: Facing volatile fossil fuel costs, Taiwanese mills (Cheng Loong, YFY, Long Chen) achieve >45–50% Solid Recovered Fuel substitution, locking steam generation costs at NT$710–760/ton and securing a major margin moat over EU peers reliant on natural gas.

Pulp Peak Season Asian Wood Pulp Market Enters "Golden Sept / Silver Oct" Peak Season Restocking: Mainstream Chinese port pulp inventories decline for the 7th consecutive week to 2.184M tonnes; downstream paper operating rates rebound to 67.2%; imported NBSK spots firm up to 4,820 RMB/ton (USD 675–680), with SHFE futures basis narrowing to healthy parity.

  9. EU Regulation EU PPWR in Force for 16 Days with Strict PFAS Audits: Customs across EU member states enforce <50 ppm total fluorine thresholds on food contact packaging, accelerating orders for water-based bio-barrier board suppliers.
  10. China Standard China GB 4806.10 Food Contact Coating Standard Mandatory on Sept 1 (4-Day Countdown): Prohibiting hazardous PFAS chemicals across China, driving nationwide food packaging substitution toward aqueous dispersion barrier coatings.
