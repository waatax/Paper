7. **Global Micro and Nanocellulose 2026–2036 Strategic Roadmap Highlights NPI and Oji Commercialization**: Major market analysis reveals accelerating adoption of Microfibrillated Cellulose (MFC) and Cellulose Nanofibers (CNF). Nippon Paper (cellenpia®) and Oji Holdings (AUROVISCO) lead efforts to commercialize bio-based barrier coatings that replace PFAS and plastic laminates.
8. **Cheng Loong's Vietnam Mill Runs at 1M MT Full Capacity; North & South Converting Expansion Captures Q4 Peak**: Cheng Loong's Binh Duong paper mill achieved full capacity utilization, while automated converting facilities in Northern and Southern Vietnam expand to meet seasonal surges from footwear, apparel, and electronics brands.
