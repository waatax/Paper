## I. Executive 10-Line Summary & Key Insights

1. **North American Containerboard Firm Seller's Market Established**: Following Packaging Corporation of America (PCA)'s announced +$140/short ton hike, International Paper (+$80/t) and Smurfit Westrock (+$100/t) continue pushing realization, driven fundamentally by 3.9M MT of permanent Americas mill capacity closures.
2. **European Structural Consolidation & Elevated Packaging Board**: Stora Enso and UPM continue closing unprofitable graphic paper lines across Europe, pivoting to premium food packaging board; Smurfit Westrock's European containerboard surged +€120/MT in Q2, rapidly tightening the transatlantic market.
3. **Chinese 4.0M MT Surplus Capacity Seeking Overseas Outlets**: 12.0M MT of cumulative containerboard capacity added between 2022-2025 cannot be fully absorbed domestically, prompting giants like Nine Dragons and Lee & Man to expand aggressively into Southeast Asia and the Middle East.
4. **Asian Virgin Pulp Bottom Consolidation & Extreme Spread Narrowing**: China imported NBSK softwood pulp averaged RMB 4,826/MT ($675/MT) in early August, while BHKP hardwood stood at RMB 4,501/MT ($628/MT). The spread collapsed to just $45/MT (RMB 325/MT, far below the $168 historical average), eliminating hardwood substitution incentives and providing solid bottom support for NBSK.
5. **Japanese Natural Disasters Disrupt Specialty Supply Chains**: The July Kumamoto earthquake forced emergency shutdowns at Nippon Paper's Yatsushiro mill, impacting specialty papers and high-end packaging; Asian converters must monitor delivery schedules and contract manufacturing alternatives.
6. **US OCC Export Prices Hold Steady at Highs**: US #11 OCC export FAS prices held steady at USD 130–135/short ton. With elevated maritime freight curbing aggressive overseas bidding, paper mill Raw Material Spreads widened substantially.
7. **China "Anti-Involution" Industrial Paper Price Hikes Take Effect**: Mill-wide price hikes of +RMB 200/MT ($28/MT) on ivory board and +RMB 50/MT on fluting led by tier-1 mills since July 1 are taking root, supported by strict energy consumption quotas under GB 31825-2024.
8. **Taiwanese Top 4 Paper Mills Performance Diverges**: Long Chen benefited from its Chinese operations turning profitable with June revenue leaping +44.10% YoY ($146M USD) to a 4-year high; Cheng Loong successfully passed through a +10% domestic hike; YFY maintained steady dual-engine growth; Chung Hwa Pulp continued expanding PFAS-free barrier products.
9. **Dual EU Packaging & Environmental Regulations Enter Critical Countdown**: EU PPWR becomes mandatory on 2026-08-12 (7 days out) for all packaging imported into the EU; the July BPA ban under (EU) 2024/3190 explicitly covers printing inks and adhesives.
10. **Carbon Fees and EUDR Supply Chain Traceability Take Root**: With Taiwan's carbon fee scheduled for 2026, mills are accelerating biomass and renewable power deployments; the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) is 147 days away, mandating GPS parcel-level Due Diligence Statements (DDS).

## VII. Global Packaging Regulations & Sustainability

### 7.1 European Union
- **PPWR (Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation)**: **Mandatory on 2026-08-12 (7 days remaining)**. All packaging imported into the EU must satisfy Design for Recycling (DfR) criteria.
- **BPA Ban (EU) 2024/3190**: Transition expired in July 2026. **Explicitly covers printing inks, coatings, adhesives, silicones, and rubber**.
- **PFHxA Restriction**: **Effective 2026-10-11 (67 days remaining)**. Prohibits PFAS in food packaging greaseproofing (25 ppb limit).
- **EUDR Deforestation Regulation**: **Effective 2026-12-30 (147 days remaining)**. Mandates GPS plot-level Due Diligence Statements (DDS).

### 7.2 United States & China
- **US State-Level PFAS Bans**: Illinois and Maine ban intentionally added PFAS across food packaging, including inks and labels.
- **China GB 4806.10-2025 Food Contact Coatings**: **Effective 2026-09-02 (28 days remaining)**. Strictly regulates paper coatings and liners.
- **China Energy Quota GB 31825-2024**: Limits energy consumption across 16 paper categories, eliminating inefficient capacity.
